argument-hint: '[site, line or area]'

Use the `plant-health-summary` skill to summarise how the Nimbit workspace is doing now.

Scope: $ARGUMENTS (if empty, the whole workspace).

Follow the skill: start with `get_workspace`, locate the scope with `browse_hierarchy` and `find_things`, read the current state with `get_latest_values` and `query_fleet`, and check `list_incidents` and `list_events`. Lead with what needs attention. Change nothing.
